# Data Attributions

The sample datasets included in this repository are adapted from published academic studies. Thanks to the original authors for making their research open and reproducible.

## Biology Track (Vertebrate Ecology Data)
The mammal trait tables and corresponding phylogenetic tree files are sourced from the global vertebrate analysis published by Munstermann et al.
* Citation: Munstermann, M.J., Heim, N.A., McCauley, D.J., Payne, J.L., Upham, N.S., Wang, S.C., & Knope, M.L. 2022. A global ecological signal of extinction risk in terrestrial vertebrates. *Conservation Biology*, 36(3), e13852. https://doi.org/10.1111/cobi.13852.
* Data: Munstermann, Maya; Heim, Noel; McCauley, Douglas; Payne, Jonathan; Upham, Nathan; Wang, Steve; Knope, Matthew. 2021. *A global ecological signal of extinction risk in terrestrial vertebrates [Dataset].* Dryad Digital Repository. https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.4b8gthtcq.

## Linguistics Track (Typological Language Data)
The linguistic trait data matrices and compressed structural language trees are built using geographical coordinates and structural features from the Grambank database compiled by Verkerk et al.
* Citation: Verkerk, A., Shcherbakova, O., Haynie, H.J., Skirgård, H., Rzymski, C., Atkinson, Q.D., Greenhill, S.J., & Gray, R.D. 2026. Enduring constraints on grammar revealed by Bayesian spatiophylogenetic analyses. *Nat Hum Behav* 10, 126–136. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-025-02325-z.
* Data: Via GitHub at [TestingLinguisticUniversals](https://github.com/SimonGreenhill/TestingLinguisticUniversals).

## Culture Track (Socio-Cultural Evolution Data)
The regional literature matrix and historical grouping records are adapted from the regional cross-cultural dataset published by Baumard et al.
* Citation: Baumard, N., Huillery, E., Hyafil, A. & Safra, L. 2022. The cultural evolution of love in literary history. *Nature Human Behavior* 6, 506–522. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-022-01292-z.
* Data: Via OSF at https://osf.io/ud35x.
